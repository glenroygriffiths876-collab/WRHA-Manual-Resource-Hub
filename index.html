What is the WRHA Manual & Resource Hub?

The hub brings together publicly accessible national clinical guidelines, health-system policies, professional learning opportunities and source-linked facility information in one organised, searchable interface.

It is designed with the Western Regional Health Authority's service context in mind while making relevant national MOHW resources easier to find.

Our approach

  • Preserve issuing authority and original document attribution.
  • Provide embedded reading and source/download options when technically supported.
  • Flag incomplete or historically dated material rather than presenting it as verified current guidance.
  • Make resources accessible on mobile devices and modest network connections.

Important governance note

This development preview does not imply institutional approval, clinical endorsement or confirmation of a document's latest status. Refer to the latest officially approved guidance and your organisation's policies.
