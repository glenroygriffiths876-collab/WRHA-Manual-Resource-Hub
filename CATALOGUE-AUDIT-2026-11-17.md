# WRHA public catalogue audit — 17 November 2026

## Scope and outcome
- Before: 79 manual-library entries, 31 legal-library entries.
- After: 136 manual-library entries, 56 legal-library entries; 192 total.
- New: 57 manual-library entries (53 PDF-linked, 4 source pages), 25 legal-library entries (21 PDF-linked, 4 statute pages).
- Four existing legal entries corrected or upgraded to direct PDF references. Three became PDF rather than source-page records.
- 29 new archived manual-library entries. The older 2009 surveillance manual is explicitly superseded by July 2026.
- No new duplicate normalized URLs or IDs; source and status on all newly added records.
- Document bodies not mirrored or modified. Metadata points to public publisher/agency URLs. Some PDF endpoints require direct access, may be CORS-restricted, or may not currently return a PDF; this audit did not perform an end-to-end browser rendering test for every source. Existing availability sorting and publisher fallback remain.
- Dates represent stated editions/revisions when ascertainable, otherwise null. A file's publication/upload date alone is not proof of its edition. Legal copies are not a certification of current consolidated law.
- The 2013 standalone tobacco instrument overlaps the compiled Public Health subsidiary legislation intentionally, for discoverability. It must be read together with amendments. A proposed 2020 Tobacco Control Bill is not presented as enacted law.

## Search scope
Official MOHW medical professional resources, policy/procedure and publications; Standards and Regulation; NCDIP guidance, IEC resources and strategic plans; national HIV/STI/TB policy and manuals; MPox and COVID-19 protocol archives; NHF provider/research resources; PCA; MOJ revised statutes and subsidiary legislation, Parliament and relevant official agencies. Searches also included nursing, maternity, mental health, dental care, community health, public health inspection, blood safety and clinical standards.

## Gaps identified
MOHW announced the revised Neonatal Care Units Policies, Procedures and Protocols Manual, Neonatal Parent Handbook and Emergency Obstetrics Protocols in February 2026. Full public copies were not identified. The neonatal manual and obstetrics publication notices are source-page entries, not substitute PDFs. The neonatal parent handbook is mentioned in the neonatal publication notice.
MOHW's 2022 register is a catalogue of Resource Centre holdings and does not establish that every listed title is downloadable. Obtain public/authorized copies of Family Health Manual, Community Health Aides Manual, Nursing Policy Manual, environmental health reporting procedure and other internal/physical holdings through the Ministry's Resource Centre when available. Word-only community/private facility guidance is linked via its official source page.
Two COVID publisher-listed links (vaccine plan and discharge protocol) could not be fully retrieved during research; their access status is qualified. Any unavailable source requires further review rather than invented document copies.
