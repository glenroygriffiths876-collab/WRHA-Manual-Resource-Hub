# Owner-authorized local publication
The repository owner explicitly confirmed permission to host all public catalogue documents on 17 November 2026. The publication workflow now stores verified source PDF bytes in documents/library, original non-PDF files in documents/originals, and a centralized checksum/page-count/source manifest. No private data or restricted account source is included. The reader prioritizes the local PDF exclusively when its catalogue record matches. Alternate editions carry source and currency notices; no invented substitute. Converted reader copies are marked pending visual review, not asserted identical. Genuine web-only collections stay collections. Existing documents are preserved. Unrecovered entries remain documented, not claimed working. The earlier archive-only workflow is superseded by this publication operation.
